Neural Plasticity &
Memory Systems

The biological processes by which the brain reorganizes itself by forming new neural connections, serving as the fundamental mechanism for learning and memory formation.

psychiatry

AI Distilled Notes

bolt Long-Term Potentiation (LTP)

A persistent strengthening of synapses based on recent patterns of activity. These are patterns of synaptic activity that produce a long-lasting increase in signal transmission between two neurons.

  • Requires simultaneous pre- and postsynaptic depolarization (Hebbian principle: "Cells that fire together, wire together").
  • Crucially depends on the activation of NMDA receptors and the subsequent influx of Calcium (Ca2+) ions.

memory The SNARE Complex

A large protein complex mediating vesicle fusion with the target membrane, essential for neurotransmitter release into the synaptic cleft.

  • Key proteins: Synaptobrevin (v-SNARE), Syntaxin, and SNAP-25 (t-SNAREs).
  • Acts as a molecular motor to pull opposing membranes together, preceding the structural changes of plasticity.

account_tree Synaptic Pruning

The process of synapse elimination that occurs between early childhood and the onset of puberty. It is highly dependent on environmental experiences and learning, ensuring the neural network becomes more efficient.
