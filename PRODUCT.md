# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Serious learners, students, and professionals who value their time and need to bridge the gap between passive reading and active recall. They need to extract key topics from study materials (PDFs/notes) and study them efficiently using spaced repetition.

## Product Purpose
DistillLearn transforms dense study materials into actionable, structured learning paths. It utilizes Gemini AI for smart ingestion/distillation and the ts-fsrs algorithm to schedule reviews at mathematically optimal times to maximize long-term retention.

## Brand Personality
Minimalist, Expert, and Focused. Highly professional, zero-distraction layout, prioritizing readability, clarity, and rapid workflow interactions. Uses sophisticated typography and a clean, high-performance UI.

## Anti-references
- AI templates (avoid beige/cream paper backgrounds, generic card grids, and tiny uppercase eyebrows above headings).
- Distracting gamification (no cartoonish badges, loud colors, or over-stimulating animations).
- Generic admin dashboards (avoid default bootstrap-like layouts, plain gray boxes, and standard blue/cyan highlights).

## Design Principles
- **Readability is Paramount**: Text contrast, line length (65–75ch), and typographic scale are optimized to prevent cognitive fatigue during long study sessions.
- **Low-Friction Efficiency**: Interventions, reviews, and edits should require minimal clicks and cognitive overhead.
- **Sophisticated Precision**: High-quality visual styling with subtle micro-animations and clean layouts that inspire confidence and academic discipline.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict adherence to WCAG AA/AAA contrast guidelines (especially on muted elements and placeholders).
- Full respect for user preferences including dark/light mode and reduced-motion settings.
